Privacy policy

Last updated June 20, 2026

Sages Services respects your privacy. This policy explains what happens when you visit sages.services or contact us by email.

Information you choose to send

This website does not submit contact forms to a server or save form entries in a database. The project brief on the home page prepares an email and opens your chosen email application. Nothing is sent until you choose to send that email.

If you contact us, your message may include your name, business name, email address, project details, and any other information you provide. We use that information to reply, assess your request, prepare a proposal, deliver requested services, and maintain appropriate business records.

Website and hosting data

The site is static and does not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or user accounts. Like most hosting services, our web host may process basic technical logs such as an IP address, browser type, requested page, timestamp, and security events. These logs support reliable delivery, security, and troubleshooting and are managed according to the host's retention practices.

Email providers and links

When you select an email link, your device and email provider handle the message. Their privacy terms apply to that activity. Links to external websites are also governed by the privacy practices of those sites.

How information is protected

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards for business communications and records. No email or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure, so please do not send passwords, payment card details, or other highly sensitive information by ordinary email.

Retention and your choices

We keep business correspondence only as long as needed to answer your request, provide services, meet legal obligations, and maintain reasonable business records. You may ask to access, correct, or delete personal information in our control, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to contact@sages.services.
