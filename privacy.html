Privacy policy

Last updated June 23, 2026

Sages Services respects your privacy. This policy explains what happens when you visit sages.services or contact us through the project form or by email.

Information you choose to send

The project form sends the information you enter to Sages Services through Web3Forms, a third-party form delivery provider. Web3Forms processes the submission so it can be delivered to our email inbox. Please do not include passwords, payment card details, or other highly sensitive information.

If you contact us, your message may include your name, business name, email address, service interest, budget range, timeline, project details, and any other information you provide. We use that information to reply, assess your request, prepare a proposal, deliver requested services, and maintain appropriate business records.

Website and hosting data

The site is static and does not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or user accounts. Like most hosting services, our web host may process basic technical logs such as an IP address, browser type, requested page, timestamp, and security events. These logs support reliable delivery, security, and troubleshooting and are managed according to the host's retention practices.

Form, email providers, and links

Form submissions are processed by Web3Forms and email is handled by our email provider. Their privacy terms apply to that processing. Links to external websites are governed by the privacy practices of those sites.

How information is protected

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards for business communications and records. No email or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure, so please do not send passwords, payment card details, or other highly sensitive information by ordinary email.

Retention and your choices

We keep business correspondence only as long as needed to answer your request, provide services, meet legal obligations, and maintain reasonable business records. You may ask to access, correct, or delete personal information in our control, subject to applicable legal requirements.
